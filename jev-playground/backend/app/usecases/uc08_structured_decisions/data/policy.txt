EXPENSE POLICY
- Meals: up to $50 per person per day. Client meals are allowed up to $120 per person if the clients' names and the business purpose are stated.
- No alcohol, except at client meals.
- Flights: economy class only. Hotels: up to $200 per night.
- Software and equipment over $100 need a manager's approval beforehand. Without it, send the claim to the manager.
- Every expense over $25 needs a receipt.
- Expenses must be submitted within 30 days.
